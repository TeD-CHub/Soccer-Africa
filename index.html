Changing Lives: Okhanya Joel & Rason Chitu Secure Football Scholarships to Hospital Hill School

Grade 10 Full Scholarship Brokered by Coach Arnold Maina Community Impact

Soccer Africa Academy is immensely proud to announce a monumental breakthrough for our youth program: our players Okhanya Joel and Rason Chitu have earned prestigious football and academic scholarships to Hospital Hill High School for Grade 10!

Changing Lives Through Football: At Soccer Africa, our core mission is transforming potential into lifelong success. An education football scholarship is a life-changing milestone that represents the culmination of years of grueling practice, relentless discipline, and deep-seated passion from grassroots community pitches to premier high school halls.

Giving Back to Our Community: We believe that every child, regardless of background, deserves a pathway to greatness. Soccer Africa actively invests in local talent, giving back by offering structured mentorship, elite training, and life skills that open doors to top-tier academic institutions and professional development.

Dedicated Mentorship: This opportunity was brokered and championed by Coach Arnold Maina (assistant coach at Leads United and Soccer Africa coach). His commitment exemplifies our philosophy of standing by our athletes every step of the way.

"An education football scholarship is a life-changing milestone which represents the culmination of years of grueling practices, relentless dedication, and a fantastic way of changing lives and inspiring the next generation of athletes. The real work begins now — Joel and Rason, earn your place in the team!"

— Coach Arnold Maina & Soccer Africa Academy
